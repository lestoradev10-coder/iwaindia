Annual Program 2025

IWA
Date : 2025 February 12-17

Location : West Bengal & Assam

We are excited to announce IWA India Foundation's Annual Program 2025, a week of impactful milestones and educational development initiatives.

Highlights of the Program

Inauguration Ceremonies

6 Houses under the IWA Roof project for underprivileged families.

10 Water Projects under IWA Pure Water to ensure clean and safe drinking water.

First Floor of HIRA School of Integrated Studies, enhancing educational infrastructure.

First Floor of HIRA Y Home, supporting orphans with better living and learning facilities.



Journey ofEducational Development

As part of the program, a transformative journey will be conducted across West Bengal and Assam, focusing on educational development in multiple villages.




Leadership &Participation

The journey will be led by our esteemed Founder and Director, Dr. Muhammed Farooq Naeemi, along with many distinguished leaders and special guests who will inspire and contribute to our vision of empowering communities through education and development.



Join us as we celebrate progress and take significant steps toward building a brighter future for the underprivileged. Stay tuned for more updates and coverage of this transformative event!
