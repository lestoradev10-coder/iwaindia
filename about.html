Welcome to IWA

A world where poverty
will not exists

IWA India Foundation is a dedicated non-profit organisation, founded by Dr. PA Muhammed Farooq Naeemi in 2013, driven by a mission to empower underprivileged communities and create sustainable solutions for a better tomorrow. Founded with a vision of fostering inclusivity and equity, we focus on transformative initiatives across education, health, livelihood development, poverty eradication, disaster relief, and community development. Our work is rooted in compassion, integrity, and a commitment to making a tangible difference in the lives of those in need.


With a special emphasis on the underserved regions of North East India, we implement innovative programs that address pressing social challenges. From providing education to children in remote villages through initiatives like School on Wheels to ensuring food security and healthcare for vulnerable populations, every project reflects our dedication to uplifting and empowering communities. By collaborating with individuals, institutions, and organisations, we strive to expand our reach and amplify our impact, ensuring transparency and accountability in all our efforts.


At IWA India Foundation, we believe in the power of collective action to transform lives. Together, with your support, we can continue building a future where every individual has the opportunity to lead a dignified and fulfilling life.

Our Vision

The IWA india Foundation's vision for education and welfare in India could be to create a society that provides equitable access to quality education, healthcare, and basic needs, empowering individuals and communities to lead prosperous and fulfilling lives.The foundation may aim to achieve this by collaborating with government agencies, NGOs, and other stakeholders to develop innovative and sustainable solutions that address the root causes of poverty and inequality, promote education and skill development, and improve healthcare and social services. In pursuing this vision, the foundation may prioritize initiatives that empower marginalized communities, such as women, children, and minorities, and leverage technology and digital platforms to enhance access to education and other essential services. Overall, the IWA india Foundation's vision would be to create a society where everyone has equal opportunities to thrive and contribute to India's growth and development.

Our Mission

Education: One of the key components of the organization's mission could be to provide quality education to underprivileged children and youth in India. This could involve working with local schools to improve access to education, providing scholarships and financial assistance to deserving students, and developing programs that promote literacy and lifelong learning.

Social Welfare: Another important aspect of the organization's mission could be social welfare initiatives. This could include working to alleviate poverty, providing access to basic needs such as food, shelter, and healthcare, and addressing social issues such as gender inequality and discrimination.

Empowerment: A key objective of the organization's mission could be to empower individuals and communities in India. This could involve providing vocational training and skill-building programs that help people gain the knowledge and tools they need to improve their economic prospects, as well as promoting self-sufficiency and resilience.

Sustainable Development: Another important aspect of the organization's mission could be a commitment to sustainable development practices. This could involve implementing environmentally friendly policies in all aspects of the organization's work, as well as promoting sustainable development practices in the communities it serves.

Collaboration: Finally, the organization's mission could include a commitment to collaboration and partnership. This could involve working closely with other NGOs, governments, and organizations to achieve common goals and address pressing social and economic challenges in India. By leveraging the resources and expertise of a diverse network of stakeholders, the organization could maximize its impact and create lasting change in the communities it serves
