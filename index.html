School on wheels

The School on Wheels project, led by the IWA India Foundation, brings mobile education to underserved communities across four states in India: Uttar Pradesh, Haryana, Bihar, and West Bengal. This initiative has reached 15 villages, supporting the education of 500 students with the help of 15 dedicated volunteers.

#Education

Street Light

The STREET LIGHT is an Street Education Project aim to address the pressing issues of inadequate limited educational opportunities in underprivileged areas across India. This initiative is designed to create a positive impact on the lives of marginalized communities by enhancing their safety and providing educational resources.
